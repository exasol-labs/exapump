## Summary
- Axes checked: 6/6
- Total findings: 15 (Blockers: 5, Advisory: 10)
- Intent Fidelity blockers: 0

Research verification: every SonarCloud fact in `decision-log.md` § Research Method was re-queried against the public API and reproduced exactly (`projectStatus.status: NONE`, `conditions: []`, `periods: []`, `coverage 55.7`, `code_smells 9`, `duplicated_lines_density 2.1`, `bugs/vulnerabilities/security_hotspots 0`, `issues.total 9` all `rust:S3776`, `effortTotal 54`). Premortem failure stories driving these findings:
1. `profile.rs` stalls near 50% because the extracted decision functions are small while the `inquire`/`rpassword` sequences stay untestable; the team then writes assertion-light tests to move the number, which task 3.2 forbids and the plan then has no path.
2. Group C runs four lanes concurrently; the `sql.rs` lane changes `write_csv`/`write_json` signatures while the `interactive.rs` lane extracts renderers calling the old ones, producing a broken merge that the "no test may be edited" rule makes hard to resolve.
3. Implementation reports done with tasks 4.2 and 4.3 checked, but neither could run: no PR analysis existed and no merge had happened. The verification report claims a green gate nobody observed.

#### [SCOPE_CREEP] ADVISORY
- Location: `plan.md` § Design/Decision "Lever 1", task 1.4; `decision-log.md` § Design Decisions [2]
- Issue: the brief asks for tests plus fixes to "satisfy the SonarCloud quality gate thresholds already configured in CI". Deleting the `unit-tests` job is not required to fix the measurement; adding `cargo llvm-cov` to `integration-tests` is. Decision [2] rejects keeping both jobs only because it is "two runs and two artifacts for one number", which is tidiness, not necessity. - Fix: In `plan.md` § Design/Decision "Lever 1" and `decision-log.md` [2], record the capability lost (container-free coverage on every PR) and state the trigger that would reinstate a container-free coverage job. Alternatively drop task 1.4, keep `unit-tests` for its fast signal, and change only which artifact the `sonar` job consumes.

## Feasibility

#### [UNSTATED_ASSUMPTION] BLOCKER
- Location: `plan.md` § Requirements row "`profile.rs` coverage", task 3.1; `decision-log.md` § Design Decisions [7]
- Issue: the whole 85% target rests on one unproven arithmetic claim. Decision [7] states "`profile.rs` at 70% supplies about 194 of them on its own, which lands overall coverage near 87%". Reaching 70% from 38.30% across roughly 611 coverable lines requires covering about 194 additional lines, so the plan needs 194 coverable lines of I/O-free decision logic to exist inside the prompt functions. The file does not obviously contain them. `src/commands/profile.rs:484-516` (`inquire_text`, `inquire_confirm`, `inquire_port`), `:517-537` (`prompt_profile_name`), `:538-553` (`prompt_new_password`), `:554-625` (`prompt_bucketfs`) and `:809-904` (`edit_bucketfs`) are dominated by `inquire::*.prompt()` and `rpassword::prompt_password` calls that stay uncovered under the "pure function taking already-collected answers" design of task 2.2. In `init` (`:370-482`) the extractable content is roughly `match args.name`, `match args.schema`, the `make_default` choice, the `Profile { .. }` literal and the default-clearing loop, and `llvm-cov` counts a multi-line struct literal as very few line records, not seventeen. No derivation of the 194 figure appears anywhere in either artifact. If the real figure is 120, `profile.rs` lands near 58%, overall lands near 81%, and both the 85% requirement and the 70% requirement fail with no stated fallback.
- Fix: Extend task 1.1 to emit a per-function uncovered-line count for `src/commands/profile.rs` from `cargo llvm-cov --json`, and add an explicit gate to `plan.md` § Implementation Tasks Phase 1: if that count shows fewer than 194 coverable lines reachable by the design chosen for task 2.2, revise the `profile.rs` and overall coverage numbers in § Requirements and in `decision-log.md` [7] before Phase 2 starts. State in § Requirements what happens when 70% is unreachable.

#### [UNSTATED_ASSUMPTION] ADVISORY
- Location: `plan.md` tasks 1.1, 1.3; `decision-log.md` § Research Method rows "Full-suite coverage"
- Issue: three different `cargo llvm-cov` invocations appear for one measurement. The 82.46% evidence came from `cargo llvm-cov --all-targets --no-fail-fast --summary-only`, task 1.1 mandates `cargo llvm-cov --lcov --output-path /tmp/lcov-baseline.info --summary-only`, and task 1.3 mandates `cargo llvm-cov --lcov --output-path lcov.info`. In task 1.2, replace the wait step's `cargo run --bin exapump -- wait` with `cargo llvm-cov run --bin exapump -- wait` or state that the double build is accepted, and add a job-level `timeout-minutes` to `integration-tests`. In task 1.3, specify `if: always()` on the `upload-artifact` step. Either delete `REQUIRE_EXASOL` in task 1.3 or add a task that makes the test suite read it.

## Requirement Quality

#### [REQUIREMENT_CONFLICT] BLOCKER
- Location: `plan.md` § Design/Patterns row "Dependency inversion on prompting" versus § Implementation Tasks task 2.2
- Issue: the plan specifies two incompatible designs for the same two functions. The Patterns table says "Dependency inversion on prompting | `profile.rs` `init` and `edit` | Business logic performs no I/O; the prompt source is supplied by the caller, so tests supply canned answers", which means injecting a prompt abstraction so the whole `init`/`edit` flow runs under test. Task 2.2 says "separate the prompt sequence from the profile-construction and profile-mutation decisions, so the decisions become pure functions taking already-collected answers", which leaves `init`/`edit` doing their own prompting and pulls out helpers beside them. The two differ in blast radius and, decisively, in coverage yield: the injected-prompt design can cover most of the 377 lines, the pure-helper design covers only the extracted subset. An implementer cannot tell which to build, and the finding above shows the 70% requirement may hold under one and fail under the other.
- Fix: Pick one design. Rewrite the `plan.md` § Design/Patterns row and task 2.2 to state the same design in the same words, and add the chosen design's name to `decision-log.md` [4] § Decision. If the injected-prompt design is chosen, state the injected abstraction's shape (trait name and the methods `init`, `edit`, `prompt_bucketfs` and `edit_bucketfs` need) in task 2.2 so the `[expert]` implementer does not redesign it.

#### [AMBIGUOUS_REQUIREMENT] BLOCKER
- Location: `plan.md` § Requirements row "Cognitive complexity"; § Implementation Tasks Phase 2 preamble; task 4.2
- Issue: "Zero open `rust:S3776` issues. Baseline 9. Every listed function at or below 15" is not verifiable during implementation. The Phase 2 preamble offers only "re-querying the Sonar issue list after the PR analysis", and task 4.2 does the same. No tool in § Dependencies, § Verification/Checklist or `scripts/check.sh` measures SonarSource cognitive complexity, and there is no local `sonar-scanner` step. So each of the seven Phase 2 tasks has a completion criterion its implementer cannot evaluate, and the plan defines no remediation loop for the case where the PR analysis still reports a function above 15. The threshold value 15 is also asserted without a source; the plan never records what the `Sonar way` Rust quality profile actually sets for `rust:S3776`.
- Fix: Add a task 1.7 to `plan.md` § Implementation Tasks Phase 1 that queries `api/rules/show?key=rust:S3776` for the configured threshold and records it in § Requirements. Add to the Phase 2 preamble one locally runnable verification per task, either a documented manual S3776 count against the published rule, or a `sonar-scanner` invocation, and name it in § Verification/Checklist. Add an explicit remediation step to task 4.2 stating what happens when the post-analysis issue count is above zero.

## Task Breakdown

#### [TASK_GRANULARITY] BLOCKER
- Location: `plan.md` § Parallelization, Group C and the sentence "Within Group C the four file lanes touch disjoint files and can run concurrently"
- Issue: the lanes are not disjoint. `src/commands/interactive.rs:7` reads `use super::sql::{error_hint, split_statements, write_csv, write_json, StatementType};`, and the plan schedules refactors of exactly those items in a different concurrent lane. Task 2.5 requires unit-testing renderers extracted from `execute_statement`, but that function's rendering arms call `write_csv` and `write_json` (`src/commands/sql.rs:364` and `:377`), which write to stdout and return `anyhow::Result<()>`; making the extracted renderers unit-testable requires changing those signatures to accept a writer, which is inside task 2.4's lane ("extract ... the per-`OutputFormat` rendering"). Task 2.6 unit-tests dispatch that consumes `split_statements`, which task 2.3 rewrites. Running these lanes concurrently produces either a broken build or a lane silently reverting the other's signature change.
- Fix: In `plan.md` § Parallelization, split Group C into two sequential groups. Put tasks 2.3 and 2.4 (`sql.rs`) in the earlier group and tasks 2.5 and 2.6 (`interactive.rs`) in the later one, keeping tasks 2.1, 2.2, 3.1 (`profile.rs`) and 2.7 (`export.rs`) concurrent with either. Add to task 2.4 the explicit instruction that it, and not task 2.5, owns any signature change to `write_csv` and `write_json`, and add to task 2.5 that it consumes those signatures unchanged. Delete the sentence claiming the four lanes touch disjoint files.

#### [TASK_GRANULARITY] BLOCKER
- Location: `plan.md` § Implementation Tasks tasks 4.2 and 4.3; § Parallelization Group D
- Issue: two of the four Phase 4 checkboxes cannot be executed by an implementer. Task 4.2 begins "After the PR analysis completes", and no PR exists while `/speq:implement` runs. Task 4.3 begins "After merge". Both sit in `## Implementation Tasks` as unqualified `- [ ]` items, so implementation completes with them either blocked or checked off without evidence. Compounding this, § Parallelization lists "Group D | 3.2, 4.1, 4.2, 4.3" as a parallel group, but the four are strictly sequential (re-measure, then run the local checklist, then read the PR analysis, then read the post-merge analysis) and Group B carries an inline "run them in order" note that Group D lacks. The plan's two headline Goals, gate `OK` on the PR analysis and on the following `main` analysis, therefore have no in-loop verification.
- Fix: Move tasks 4.2 and 4.3 out of `plan.md` § Implementation Tasks into a new `## Post-Merge Operator Checklist` section, stated as operator actions rather than checkboxes. Keep tasks 3.2 and 4.1 in Phase 4 and correct § Parallelization Group D to list only those two with the note "run in order". Add to `plan.md` § Requirements which of the acceptance criteria are verified in-loop (local `cargo llvm-cov` TOTAL and per-file figures) and which are deferred to the operator checklist.

## Design Depth

#### [INFORMATION_LEAKAGE] ADVISORY
- Location: `plan.md` tasks 2.4 and 2.5; `src/commands/interactive.rs:238-253` and `:286-301`, `src/commands/sql.rs:364,377`
- Issue: the decision "how a `RecordBatch` is rendered in format F" is reflected in three places today: `sql.rs` `run`, and twice inside `interactive.rs` `execute_statement`, where lines 238-253 and 286-301 are a verbatim duplicate of the same three-arm `match format` block. The plan extracts this decision independently in two tasks assigned to two lanes and never names an owning module, so the same decision survives in two modules after the refactor. Per `/speq:design-philosophy`, recurrence of one decision across modules is the defect that deserves the closest attention, and consolidating it is also the cheapest route to the duplication requirement.
- Fix: Add to `plan.md` § Design/Patterns a row naming one owner for batch rendering (a `render` function in `sql.rs` taking `&[RecordBatch]`, the format, and a `&mut impl Write`). Rewrite task 2.4 to create it and task 2.5 to call it, and state in task 2.5 that the duplicate arms at `interactive.rs:238-253` and `:286-301` collapse into that single call.

#### [SHALLOW_DESIGN] ADVISORY
- Location: `plan.md` task 2.4; § Consequences row "Extract logic rather than split functions mechanically"
- Issue: § Consequences commits to extraction over mechanical splitting, but task 2.4 reads as mechanical splitting: "extract the input-source selection (argument, file, stdin), the per-`OutputFormat` rendering, and the status-line construction into separate functions". A function whose whole job is building a status line is shallow; its interface costs a caller about as much as writing the line inline. Producing several such helpers to move one metric is the classitis `/speq:design-philosophy` warns against, and the plan gives no criterion for telling a real abstraction from a metric-driven split.
- Fix: Add one sentence to `plan.md` § Design/Patterns stating the acceptance test for each extraction: the extracted function must be callable from at least one site other than the function it came from, or must hide a decision the caller then stops making. Rewrite task 2.4 to drop "status-line construction" as a separate extraction unless it meets that test.
